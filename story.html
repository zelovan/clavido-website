Our story

Brew & Bindings began with two shelves: one of potions, one of books. We wanted a shop where you could learn a craft and take something home that was made with care.

Everything we sell is made in small batches, by people who can tell you how it was made.

Made by hand

Potions are brewed and books are sewn in our own workshop.

Small batches

We make twenty at a time, so each one gets our attention.

Open to beginners

Every workshop starts from zero and every question is welcome.
